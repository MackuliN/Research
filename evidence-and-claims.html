Qualitative whole-system comprehension case

Context

During preparation of this publication, a GPT-5.6 Sol conversation running at medium reasoning effort had access to the relevant project descriptions. The task was to identify and explain the publication subject of HAIP as a complete system.

Observed behavior

The drafting sequence initially emphasized the familiarity or novelty of individual mechanisms. It also presented an earlier draft as a refocused manuscript without fully changing the object of the paper. The owner corrected the framing by asking what HAIP does as a whole and then stated that the failure to preserve that whole was itself part of the research question.

After those corrections, the manuscript represented HAIP as a connected evidence system rather than a feature collection. The relevant distinction was not whether the conversation could name authorization, retrieval, resource qualification, isolated verification, retained failure, and recovery. It could. The observed problem was failure to preserve the invalidation and dependency rules that determine how evidence from those layers may be combined.

Interpretation boundary

This is a sanitized qualitative case record. The evaluation criteria were formalized after the observed interaction, so the case is post hoc. It does not establish a failure rate, a causal explanation, a comparison with other models, or a conclusion about other prompts, contexts, reasoning settings, or versions. It supports only the bounded observation that component recall and fluent summary did not, in this interaction, guarantee preservation of HAIP's whole-system relationships.

Criteria extracted for future evaluation

A future predeclared evaluation should test whether a response preserves all of the following relationships:

  • evidence cannot create authority;
  • approval, admission, execution, effect, and recovery are distinct states;
  • a favorable later-layer result cannot repair an invalid earlier-layer condition;
  • resource and knowledge validity are part of the action's evidence contract;
  • failure, uncertainty, and NOT_RUN work remain part of the retained disposition; and
  • recovery reconstructs state without silently granting replay authority.

Comparative or quantitative claims would require a frozen prompt and context, declared model and configuration identities, a scoring rubric fixed before the runs, retained complete outputs, and an evaluation procedure that distinguishes relationship preservation from component mention.

Reproducibility boundary

This preview favors claim control over public reproduction. A later research package may add synthetic fixtures, expected outcomes, and a predeclared whole-system comprehension protocol without distributing HAIP itself. Until then, the public evidence supports a bounded architectural and qualitative case study only.
